# Attribution and notices

This collection was adapted from Khaled Eniba's personal AI skills and prepared as an independent public snapshot.

- **pressure-test:** adapts the decision-tree and dependency-ordered questioning approach from Matt Pocock's [skills repository](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), originally its grilling method. This collection adds source checks, concrete consequences for alternatives, explicit human decisions and visible unresolved questions.
- **brainstorm:** its development was informed by the brainstorming approach in Jesse Vincent's [Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/tree/main/skills/brainstorming). This collection uses independent entry points and conversational exploration; it does not adopt that project's required implementation pipeline.
- **set-goal:** adapted from Khaled Eniba's personal task-scoping method.

Upstream projects do not endorse this collection. Their licence notices are retained below. The pattern explanations link to their conceptual sources separately.
